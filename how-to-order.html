Ordering

How to order

We don't take card payments online. Instead, every order is checked by hand before you pay, so you never pay for a shell that has already gone.

  1. Request your orderAdd shells to your cart and send an order request with your delivery address and preferred payment method.
  2. We confirm and send payment detailsWithin 24 hours we email you to confirm stock, with your order reference, total, and bank or wallet details. Your shells are held for days.
  3. Pay and we postPay by NZ bank transfer or stablecoin. Once payment arrives, your shells are carefully packed and sent tracked.

Frequently asked questions

How much is shipping?

A flat per order anywhere in New Zealand, tracked. Every specimen is individually wrapped and boxed.

Do you ship overseas?

Not yet. We ship to . If you're overseas, leave your email on a sold-out species or contact us and we'll let you know when international shipping opens.

How do I pay by bank transfer?

After we confirm your order, we email our NZ bank account number. Please use your order reference as the payment reference so we can match it.

How do I pay with crypto?

We accept stablecoins (USDC or USDT) only, so the price can't move between ordering and paying. We email you the wallet address, network and exact amount. Please double-check the network before sending, because payments sent on the wrong network can't be recovered.

What if I don't pay within the hold period?

After days the shells are released back into stock. Just reply to our email if you need a little more time.

How are sizes measured?

Sizes are the greatest length in millimetres, measured with calipers. Individual specimens may vary slightly from the listed size; ask if you need an exact measurement.

Can I return a shell?

If a specimen arrives damaged or isn't as described, contact us within 7 days with photos and we'll refund or replace it. Your rights under the NZ Consumer Guarantees Act always apply.

Are your shells legally and ethically sourced?

Yes. We don't sell species protected under CITES or New Zealand wildlife law without the required permits, and we can tell you the origin of any specimen on request.

A species I want is sold out. What can I do?

Open the species page and use the "Notify me" form. We'll email you when it's back.
